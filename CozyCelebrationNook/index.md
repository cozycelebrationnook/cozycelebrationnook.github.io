# Privacy Policy

CozyCelebrationNook ("I", "my") is a small shop selling original printable products on Etsy.

This policy covers the app "CozyCelebrationNook Scheduler", a personal tool I use only with my own Pinterest business account.

## What the app does
It creates boards and schedules Pins for my own products, linking to my own Etsy listings, and reads statistics of my own Pins. It is used only by me, the account owner, through Pinterest's standard sign-in (OAuth).

## Data
The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. It only handles content and statistics of my own Pinterest account. The access token is stored encrypted in the secrets of my private GitHub repository and is never shared.
